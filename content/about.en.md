A non-commercial project by one person: a careful list of {{counts.sites}} zamindar palaces, estate houses and rajbaris, what the sources say about each, and how to reach them from Dhaka. It has no accounts and keeps no tracking.

## The core book {#core-book}

The starting list comes from পূর্ববঙ্গের জমিদারবাড়ি (Purbo Bonger Jomidar Bari) by Md. Mahmud Ali (Dibyaprakash, 2024), cited by {{book.sites}} sites. Its appendix, a district-by-district list of houses, has been checked against the printed book; the chapter entries were used through transcriptions. Every site was then checked against Banglapedia, Wikipedia, newspapers, government portals and travel guides: {{counts.sources}} sources in all.

[See the book's source page](/sources/SRC-0001/) · [All sources](/sources/)

## How records are graded {#grading}

Each site carries one of four grades. It appears as a quiet badge on the site page and as the "Only well-documented sites" filter in Explore.

- **Confirmed** ({{counts.confidence.confirmed}}): written sources describe the site.
- **Partly confirmed** ({{counts.confidence.partially_confirmed}}): some details are sourced; others are not yet.
- **Unconfirmed** ({{counts.confidence.unconfirmed}}): only a list entry or a named map place exists. Kept so the place is not lost.
- **Not yet graded** ({{counts.confidence.not_graded}}): early records, still to be reviewed.

Fields we could not confirm are listed at the bottom of each site page as "Still to confirm": {{counts.stillToConfirm}} of them across the dataset.

## What "location confirmed" means {#locations}

- **Confirmed** ({{counts.locations.verified}}): several independent sources put the pin in the same place, usually within about 150 m.
- **One source** ({{counts.locations.singleSource}}): a single named place, usually a Google Maps pin. Likely right, not cross-checked.
- **Approximate** ({{counts.locations.approximate}}): the pin marks a nearby landmark such as the village bazar; the house is within a kilometre or two. The 1.5 km circle on the map is a display convention, not a measurement.
- **Unverified** ({{counts.locations.unverified}}): a pin we have not been able to check at all. It stays on the map with a "?" and the label "Location unverified".
- **No pin** ({{counts.locations.noPin}}): we could not place it. The site stays in lists and on its district page, never with a guessed pin.

## Disagreements, open questions and legends {#disagreements}

- **{{counts.disagreements}} disagreements.** When sources give different dates, founders, sizes or hours, the site page shows every version and who said it. We don't pick one quietly.
- **{{counts.openQuestions}} open questions.** A claim that only one source makes, and that we have not been able to check, is shown as an open question, not as a fact and not as a disagreement.
- **{{counts.legends}} stories, kept apart.** Local accounts and legends live in their own box, marked "not verified", and never in the history timeline.

A source is attached to a version only when the version names it. Sources that can't be matched to one version are listed with the disagreement, so the page never suggests that every source supports every claim.

**Possible duplicates: {{counts.possibleDuplicates}}.** Records that may describe the same house are kept as separate sites, each with a note, until someone checks.

## Costs, fees and dates {#costs}

- One-way costs from Dhaka are **estimates**, mostly from road distance at the BRTA bus rate published in September 2026, plus local transport.
- "No ticket reported" means just that. We never write "Free", and an unknown fee is shown as unknown, not as zero.
- Hours and fees show when they were last checked. After 12 months they are marked "May be out of date".
- Trip budgets add up only the amounts we know, and name what they leave out. There is no total.

## Not done yet {#not-done}

- No site has been visited in person, and no owner, caretaker or Department of Archaeology office has been contacted.
- No pin has been checked with a field GPS reading.
- Apart from the Manikganj loop, whose fares the project editor reported in October 2026, bus fares have not been confirmed at the counter. Accommodation, food and guide costs are not researched.
- Most opening hours and closing days are not documented yet.
